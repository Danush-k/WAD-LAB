About TechNova 2026

TechNova 2026 is a National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. The symposium provides a platform for students from various colleges to showcase their technical knowledge, creativity, innovation and problem-solving skills through various technical and non-technical events.
